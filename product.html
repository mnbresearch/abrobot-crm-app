The questions people actually ask.

Answered plainly, including the ones with awkward answers.
Is there a free trial?
Not a time-limited one, and we'd rather say why than pretend. A 7-day trial of a CRM is long enough to look around and nowhere near long enough to see whether it changes how you sell — so it mostly teaches people to rush. Instead, signing up is free and stays free. You can build your pipeline, add your fields and tune the AI agent without paying anything, and a small free allowance — 50 records and 50 AI replies — lets you put a handful of real leads through it and watch what happens. What ₹999 switches on is capturing records at volume, bringing your existing list in, AI replies at volume and sending. So you get to try the part that matters — does this fit how my business works — before any money changes hands, and you're never mid-setup when a clock runs out.
Why ₹999? What's the catch?
There isn't one, but here's the arithmetic so you can check. ₹999 is flat for the whole business — three users, 1,000 records, 1,000 AI replies a month. The comparable plan at Zoho, Professional, is listed at ₹1,400 per user per month on zoho.com as of September 2026, so an eight-person team pays ₹11,200. We can charge less because we don't have a sales team, the AI runs on models we host efficiently, and one engineer maintains the product. What Starter genuinely does not include is WhatsApp sending and the REST API — those cost us real money per message, so they start at Growth (₹2,499). We'd rather name that than have you find out after paying.
Who owns the data, and can I get it out?
You own it. Every record, every custom field and every note exports to CSV from the Reports screen, any time, at no charge — not a support ticket, not a fee, not a 30-day notice. On a paid plan the REST API reads the same data programmatically. We say this loudly because the honest test of a CRM is how easy it is to leave, and a company that makes leaving hard is telling you something about how it plans to keep you.
How hard is it to move off my current CRM or spreadsheet?
Export a CSV from wherever you are now, import it here, and map your columns on screen before anything saves. Then paste one line of code into your website for the AI agent. Most businesses are running the same day. Being straight about the order: building your pipeline and fields is free, and the free allowance covers 50 records, so you can prove the mapping on a sample — but bringing your full list across needs a paid plan, because that is the storage you're actually buying. If you'd like, send us your export first and we'll tell you honestly whether it maps cleanly, before you pay anything.
Can I cancel? What happens to my records?
There is nothing to cancel. A payment buys one month and then simply stops — we create no recurring mandate on your card, so there is no renewal to catch and no notice period or exit fee. If you don't want another month, don't pay for one. When a plan lapses the account goes read-only rather than being deleted: you keep seeing everything and can still export the lot. Nothing is destroyed because you stopped paying. Coming back later just means picking a plan again.
Are WhatsApp messages really included?
Included up to a monthly count — 1,500 on Growth, 3,000 on Business — and the reason there's a number at all is that Meta charges us per message, so "unlimited WhatsApp" from anyone is either a bluff or a bill waiting to arrive. Your usage is shown live in Settings → Plan & usage. When the allowance is spent, WhatsApp sending pauses until the reset or an upgrade — we never quietly bill you for overage. Everything else keeps working. From 1 October 2026 Meta also starts charging for service messages, which is priced into these numbers already.
Is my customers' data separated from other businesses'?
Yes, and it's enforced in the database rather than in application code — every record carries an organisation, and row-level security means a query from your account cannot return another business's rows even if the app asked it to. We test this with a script that attempts real cross-tenant reads, inserts, updates and deletes and requires every one of them to fail. Data is hosted in the cloud on Supabase (Postgres). On backups we'd rather be exact than reassuring: we take a nightly database dump and keep it for 90 days, which is a real copy but a modest one — it is not point-in-time recovery, and we have not yet rehearsed a full restore, so we don't claim a recovery time. That is also why CSV export is free and always available: your own copy is the backup you control.
Do I need a developer?
For the normal setup, no. The AI agent is one line of code pasted before </body> — the same shape as a Google Analytics tag, and your web person or site builder will recognise it instantly. Everything after that is configured from the CRM: greeting, tone, what the agent knows, your pipeline stages, your fields. A developer only becomes useful on Business, where the REST API and webhooks let you wire the CRM into other systems.
What if the AI answers something wrong?
You control what it knows and what it may not say — there's a guardrails box for exactly this, and the sensible default is that it never invents prices or promises, and offers to have a human confirm instead. Every conversation is saved in full under Conversations, so you can read what it actually said rather than hope. If it gets something wrong, you edit the knowledge and it's fixed for the next visitor. It's a well-briefed receptionist, not an oracle, and it should be set up that way.
What isn't ready yet?
White-label and multi-brand are on the roadmap and not available today, so please don't buy on that basis — ask us for timelines first. There's no native mobile app; the CRM is a web app that works on a phone browser, and lead alerts arrive on Telegram, which is what makes the 60-second number real. If something you need is missing, ask before you pay and we'll tell you straight whether it exists, whether it's coming, or whether we aren't the right fit.
