AbroBot CRM is an AI-powered lead-management platform (SaaS) provided by AbroBot Technologies Private Limited. It captures leads from your website, WhatsApp and forms with an AI chat agent, pushes them to your phone in real time, and manages your whole pipeline. Below are our plans. Signing up is free and takes a minute. The free plan is small but real — 50 records in total, plus 50 AI replies and 20 emails a month — so you can put the AI agent on your own site and watch an enquiry arrive before you pay anything. A paid plan is what lifts the ceiling and adds WhatsApp sending and the API. Starter is ₹999/month, which is the plan to start on. All payments are processed securely in INR via our Cashfree PCI-DSS compliant gateway.

Some things are the same on every plan, and we'd rather say so than dress them up as tier features: the AI agent and its knowledge, guardrails and tone are fully editable on every plan including free; so are the reports and the team leaderboard. What the tiers actually buy you is capacity — users, records, AI replies, emails, WhatsApp messages and automations — plus the REST API and webhooks on Business.

Support. We are a small team, not a helpdesk. There is no ticketing system, no support queue and no service-level agreement — what there is, is a person who answers. Email contact@mnbresearch.com or WhatsApp +91 97114 88480 on any plan, including free, and you will hear back within 1 business day. We'll help you get set up over a call if you want one. That is a human commitment rather than a product feature, and it is the same on every plan.

Allowances (AI replies, emails, WhatsApp messages) reset on the 1st of each month and are shown live in Settings → Plan & usage. When one runs out that feature pauses until the reset or an upgrade — nothing is deleted and no overage is charged without you choosing it. WhatsApp messages are billed by Meta per message, which is why they are counted.

Pricing notes. All prices are in INR (₹), billed monthly, and are exclusive of GST unless stated. Subscriptions do not renew automatically. Each payment buys one fixed period and then simply ends — no recurring mandate is created against your card, so there is nothing to cancel and no renewal to catch. When a period ends the account becomes read-only until you choose to pay again; nothing is deleted. On GST: we do not yet issue an automated invoice. You get a payment confirmation from our gateway at checkout, and a GST invoice on request — email contact@mnbresearch.com with your GSTIN and we will send one. Annual billing (save ~2 months) and volume/education discounts are available on request. The price shown at checkout is the price you pay — no hidden charges.
