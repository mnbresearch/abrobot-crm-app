AbroBot CRM is an AI-powered lead-management platform (SaaS) provided by AbroBot Technologies Private Limited. It captures leads from your website, WhatsApp and forms with an AI chat agent, pushes them to your phone in real time, and manages your whole pipeline. Below are our plans and add-ons. Signing up is free and takes a minute — you can set up your pipeline, fields and AI agent before paying. Capturing records, AI replies and sending switch on when you choose a plan. Starter is ₹999/month, which is the plan to start on. All payments are processed securely in INR via our Cashfree PCI-DSS compliant gateway.
