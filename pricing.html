AbroBot CRM is an AI-powered lead-management platform (SaaS) provided by AbroBot Technologies Private Limited. It captures leads from your website, WhatsApp and forms with an AI chat agent, pushes them to your phone in real time, and manages your whole pipeline. Below are our plans and add-ons. Every plan includes a 7-day free trial. All payments are processed securely in INR via our Cashfree PCI-DSS compliant gateway.
