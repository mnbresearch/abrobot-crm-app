AbroBot CRM is an AI-powered lead-management platform (SaaS) provided by AbroBot Technologies Private Limited. It captures leads from your website, WhatsApp and forms with an AI chat agent, pushes them to your phone in real time, and manages your whole pipeline. Below are our plans and add-ons. New accounts start on a 7-day free trial (2 users, 100 records) with no card required. All payments are processed securely in INR via our Cashfree PCI-DSS compliant gateway.
